Privacy Policy

TikTok Copy Paste (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 2026-09-30

This extension helps you download public TikTok profile videos to your computer and re-upload/schedule them through TikTok Studio in your browser. It is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.

Data we access

  • TikTok pages you open - to list videos on a profile and to automate the Studio upload UI while you are logged in.
  • Video files and captions - downloaded to your Chrome Downloads folder; captions may be saved in a local Excel (.xlsx) file you create.
  • Extension settings & Pro status - stored locally in Chrome storage (folder name, theme, daily usage counters). Paid status is also synced via ExtensionPay when you upgrade.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate an analytics backend for this extension by default. Video files, captions, cookies, and TikTok account credentials are not uploaded to our servers. Pro checkout and subscription status are handled by ExtensionPay (and Stripe through ExtensionPay). ExtensionPay may process your email for payment and login.

Permissions

  • downloads - save videos and spreadsheets to your Downloads folder.
  • storage - remember settings, queue state, and Pro status.
  • scripting / tabs - inject helpers on TikTok tabs you use.
  • offscreen - reliably save downloaded media blobs.
  • Host access to TikTok CDN domains - fetch video media with your session cookies.
  • Content script on extensionpay.com - detect successful checkout so Pro unlocks immediately.

Contact

For privacy questions, email the publisher address listed on the Chrome Web Store listing (configure supportEmail in the extension monetization settings).
